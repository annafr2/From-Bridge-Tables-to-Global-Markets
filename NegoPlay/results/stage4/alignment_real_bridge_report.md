## Caveats (bridge-expert review)
- **n = 5 profiles → low power** (p not significant); ρ is an indication.
- **Selection confound:** the Slam Hunter profile is defined by *bidding* slams, so it may partly proxy overall player strength, not pure style.
- **Negotiation is simulated** — no real negotiation data exists.
- Partnership credit is shared equally between partners (unavoidable from this data; a bridge result is a partnership outcome).

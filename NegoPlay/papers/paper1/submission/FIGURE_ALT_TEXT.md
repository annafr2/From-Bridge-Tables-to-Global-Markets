# Alternative Text (Alt Text) for All Figures

Prepared per the Springer accessibility requirement ("please provide
suitable alternative text or descriptions for accessibility purposes").
All five figures are the author's own work, generated by the scripts in

---

**Fig. 1 — radar_profiles.png**
Radar chart with eight behavioral feature axes, shown as z-scores. Five
overlaid polygons represent the four extreme profiles and the Generalist
baseline. Each profile's polygon spikes on its own defining axis — Slam
Hunter on slam rate, Insurance Player on partscore rate, Fighter on
penalty-double rate, NT Specialist on no-trump rate — and stays close to
the baseline on all other axes.

**Fig. 2 — real_skill_spectrum.png**
Horizontal spectrum of bridge skill measured in IMPs per board relative
to the two-table datum. The random ZI-C-style baseline sits at the far
left at about minus 7 IMP per board; the elite-derived profiles cluster
near zero; the double-dummy oracle sits at the right at plus 1.1 IMP per
board. The ordering random, then human, then omniscient validates the
metric.

**Fig. 3 — negotiation_example.png**
Line chart of one negotiation scenario, acquiring a SaaS startup. The
horizontal axis shows negotiation rounds one to four; the vertical axis
shows the buyer's offer in millions of dollars. Three dashed reference
lines mark the seller's opening ask at 13, the fair value at 9, and the
seller's floor at 8. Five colored lines trace the agents' offers:
aggressive profiles anchor near the floor at 8 and hold; cautious
profiles concede upward toward 11.

**Fig. 4 — style_alignment.png**
Scatter plot with five labeled points, one per profile. The horizontal
axis is bridge-side aggression as a composite z-score; the vertical axis
is negotiation-side demand depth. Points rise from the lower left
(Insurance Player, Generalist) to the upper right (Fighter), showing a
positive rank correlation of plus 0.80.

**Fig. 5 — style_transfer_control.png**
Two-panel comparison of the inverse-prompt control. Left panel: in the
normal condition, bridge aggression and negotiation demand depth
correlate at plus 0.80. Right panel: when each profile keeps its label
but receives the opposite profile's bridge-derived skills, the
correlation flips to minus 0.90 — the Fighter becomes softer and the
Insurance Player becomes harder.
